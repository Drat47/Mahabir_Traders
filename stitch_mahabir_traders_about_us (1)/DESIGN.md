## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a robust, architectural, and deeply dependable visual presence tailored for heavy-duty building materials, structural goods, luxury sanitaryware, and architectural tiling. The target audience comprises commercial contractors, civil engineers, architects, interior designers, and individual homeowners seeking reliable, professional-grade building solutions.

The aesthetic philosophy draws heavily from **Modern Industrialism** merged with **Architectural Minimalism**:
- **Tone:** Grounded, structural, highly dependable, tactile, and clear.
- **Visual Tension:** Dense structural elements (steel beams, bulk cement sacks, precise tiles) offset by expansive architectural breathing room and high-clarity typography.
- **Physicality:** Textures are celebrated via high-resolution photography, framed within razor-clean edges, precise micro-borders, and tactile structural surfaces that inspire confidence in structural integrity and material durability.

## Colors

The palette balances structural solidity with vibrant trade accents:
- **Primary (`#0F1E36` - Deep Iron Navy):** Evokes structural ironwork, corporate durability, and authoritative engineering integrity. Applied to dominant text, deep headers, and key callouts.
- **Secondary (`#C2410C` - Terracotta Brick Red):** Evokes clay bricks, fired ceramics, safety, and craftsmanship. Used for primary interactive triggers, primary CTA buttons, and highlighted product tags.
- **Tertiary (`#2563EB` - Industrial Blue):** Carried over from core brand trade origins, signaling precise plumbing, sanitaryware specs, and digital utility links.
- **Neutral (`#64748B` - Slate Concrete):** Provides calibrated mid-tone contrast for meta-specifications, technical dimensions, secondary text, and divider baselines.

### Functional Surface Roles
- Canvas surfaces sit on a clean off-white mineral base (`#F8FAFC`), while interactive product containers utilize pure white (`#FFFFFF`) to ensure tile glazes, chrome sanitary fittings, and stone finishes retain uncorrupted color reproduction.
- Status and validation rely on `#16A34A` for stock availability/dispatch confirmations and `#D97706` for bulk freight notices or batch-order quotes.

## Typography

The type pairing merges industrial precision with highly legible commercial utility:
- **Display & Headlines (`Space Grotesk`):** Delivers a technical, engineered aesthetic reminiscent of architectural blueprints and industrial manufacturing stamps. Its disciplined geometric cadence reinforces strength when framing heavy product categories like TMT Steel, PPC/OPC Cement, and Precision Vitrified Tiles.
- **Body & Functional UI (`Work Sans`):** Provides exceptional rhythm and clarity across high-density data tables, inventory sheets, logistics breakdowns, and technical specification labels.
- **Spec Labels & Numerical Metrics (`spec-code`):** Employs tracked uppercase Space Grotesk for SKU codes, metric grades (e.g., Fe-550D, Grade 53), and load ratings.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded on a disciplined 12-column fluid grid system across desktop resolutions, consolidating to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile:
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** Max container width capped at 1440px with `2.5rem` outer canvas padding and `1.5rem` gutters. Ideal for multi-column product comparisons, specification sheets, and structured mega-catalogs.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** Columns reflow to 2 or 3 items per row with unified 1.25rem gutters. Filter bars shift into collapsible slide-out drawer sheets.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stacking for heavy content cards and 2-column configurations for tile/fittings catalog grids. Sticky bottom utility bar anchors bulk quotation requests and instantaneous calls to dispatch.
- **Spacing Rhythm:** Based on an 8pt step system (`0.25rem`, `0.5rem`, `1rem`, `1.5rem`, `2.5rem`). Component padding never exceeds layout gutter sizes, preserving structural proportion.

## Elevation & Depth

To sustain the architectural and physical integrity of the building materials sector, this design system avoids superficial, diffuse floating dropshadows. Depth is articulated via **Tonal Planar Stacking** coupled with **Low-Contrast Precision Outlines**:

1. **Surface Tier 0 (Canvas):** Tone `#F8FAFC`. The foundational job site floor.
2. **Surface Tier 1 (Cards, Modules):** Tone `#FFFFFF` with an architectural hairline border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`). Flat elevation with zero blur shadow under resting states.
3. **Interactive Hover / Focus State:** Surface shifts subtly upwards with a razor-thin structural shadow: `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 30, 54, 0.08), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 30, 54, 0.04)`, alongside an accent border shift to Terracotta (`#C2410C`).
4. **Modal & Floating Panels (Quotations, Spec Zoomers):** Supported by a clean, neutral tinted backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(4px); background-color: rgba(15, 30, 54, 0.4)`) and an architectural structural shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 30, 54, 0.15), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 30, 54, 0.1)`.

## Shapes

The design system embraces a **Soft / Engineering Precision (`1`)** shape language:
- **Base Components (Buttons, Input Fields, Badges):** Sculpted with a crisp `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius, reflecting milled steel, cut stone, and cleanly aligned bricks.
- **Card Containers & Modules (`rounded-lg`):** Limited to `0.5rem` (8px). Corners remain distinctly intentional rather than round or whimsical.
- **Overlays, Drawers & Modals (`rounded-xl`):** `0.75rem` (12px), softening full-screen interactions while preserving geometric order.
- **Zero Pill Shapes:** Pill shapes are strictly forbidden to ensure the identity remains rooted in construction, engineering, and structural masonry.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button (Order / Quote Trigger):** Background Terracotta Brick (`#C2410C`), text `#FFFFFF`, font `label-lg`, height 44px, border-radius 4px. On hover: `#9A3412` with a 1px downward translation.
- **Secondary Button (Download Spec Sheet / View Dimensions):** Border `1.5px solid #0F1E36`, background transparent, text `#0F1E36`. On hover: background `#0F1E36`, text `#FFFFFF`.
- **Ghost Utility Button:** Text `#334155`, transparent fill, subtle hover background `#E2E8F0`.

### Cards & Showcase Tiles
- **Product Card:** Base white surface with `1px solid #E2E8F0` border. High-aspect ratio (4:3 or 1:1) photographic container with neutral concrete backdrop (`#F1F5F9`). Contains: Category Eyebrow in `label-md` Slate (`#64748B`), Product Title in `title-md` (`#0F1E36`), Technical Specification snippet in `spec-code`, and live stock/grade badge.
- **Category Showcase Tile:** Large full-bleed architectural images featuring an anchored deep navy gradient overlay at bottom containing category name and catalog count.

### Badges & Chips
- **Specification Chips (e.g., "53 Grade", "Fe-550D", "Anti-Skid"):** Monospaced-leaning Space Grotesk `spec-code`, background `#F1F5F9`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#334155`, padding 2px 8px, border-radius 2px.
- **Status Indicator Badges:** 
  - *In Stock / Dispatch Ready:* Light emerald background (`#DCFCE7`), text `#16A34A`.
  - *Bulk Only / Factory Direct:* Light amber background (`#FEF3C7`), text `#D97706`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Text Inputs & Quantity Selectors:** Height 44px, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, background `#FFFFFF`, font `body-md`. Focus state uses a crisp `2px solid #0F1E36` outline with zero fuzzy glow. Includes integrated unit suffixes (e.g., "Bags", "Tonnes", "Sq. Ft.").

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square 18px boxes with 2px corner radius. Border `2px solid #64748B`. When active: solid fill `#0F1E36` with sharp white structural check icon.

### Technical Data Tables (Material Grade & Dimension Specs)
- Alternating subtle rows (`#FFFFFF` to `#F8FAFC`). Header row styled in deep iron navy `#0F1E36` with white text in uppercase `label-md`. Vertical borders `1px solid #E2E8F0` for accurate engineering column tracking.
